Name

YUPI

How YUPl came around

The name "YUPl" is a clever and memorableacronym that originated from the UPMC(University of Pittsburgh Medical Center)healthcare provider. The name has since beenadopted for use in general healthcare settings asa smart conversational agent for urgent care. Thename "YUPl" was conveys its positiveconnotations, memorability, and ability to evoketrust and professionalism.

How to use the term “YUP!"

  • Always capitalize all letters in "YUPl" to maintainconsistency and brand recognition.
  • When introducing "YUPl" in written materialsinclude a brief explanation of what it stands for:"Your Urgent care Personal interface" or "YourUrgent care Personal intelligence.
  • Use "YUPl" as a singular noun when referring tothe conversational agent. For example,"YUPl ishere to help you with your urgent care needs.
